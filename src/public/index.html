About Our Sacred Mission

Awakening Inner Peace & Living Stillness

Conceived as a sanctuary for mental quietude, conscious breathwork, and unconditional service to humanity.

The Guidance of Sri Vijaya Pratap Maharaj

In our hyper-connected modern existence, human beings face unprecedented levels of chronic anxiety, cognitive fatigue, and emotional fragmentation. The Art Of Relaxation was founded under the spiritual vision of Sri Vijaya Pratap Maharaj to remind every seeker that true relaxation is not merely passive rest—it is establishing oneself in profound, undisturbed inner stillness.

Rooted in authentic Vedic meditation, breath regulation (Pranayama), and selfless action (Nishkama Karma), Sri Vijaya Pratap Maharaj imparts practical spiritual wisdom that enables seekers to quiet mental turbulence, cultivate resilience, and live with boundless joy and purpose.

Together with our sister trust, Divya Yoga Mandali Charitable Trust, we bridge traditional yogic disciplines with active humanitarian outreach—ensuring that spiritual elevation translates into tangible community well-being.

“
When the mind dissolves its turbulent anxieties into the stillness of the heart, peace blossoms naturally. From that peace arises unconditional compassion and selfless service to all beings.
— Sri Vijaya Pratap Maharaj
🪷

The Dual Pillar Philosophy

Sadhana (Inner Awakening) & Seva (Selfless Action)

We believe inner peace without outer service is incomplete, and external action without internal silence leads to exhaustion. By integrating meditation with Annadanam and rural welfare, we nurture balanced, harmonious lives.
